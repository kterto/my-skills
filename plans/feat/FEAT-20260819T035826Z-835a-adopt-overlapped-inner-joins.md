## Overview

Adopt ADR-0013 in the `orchestrator` skill: replace Step 3s's **global** leaf barrier with a **per-lane** barrier so a lane's inner join begins as soon as its own sub-lanes are DONE, and — in the same phase — reprice the inner-join level in `references/config.md` from serialized `k × J` to concurrent slowest-of-`k` = `J`, charged once on the first adoption in the exact shape the Step 2s `A` bullet already uses. The barrier half and the charge half are one atomic unit of work: landing either without the other recreates the gate-and-ladder-on-two-accounts defect that *The cost side* and its worked examples exist to prevent (ADR-0013, `references/config.md` → *Worked example — the gate verdict and the ladder figure must agree*).

Beyond the swap, the plan lands the seven concurrency resolutions the spec fixes: deferred gates move wholesale from Step 3s to Step 3j item 4 (FR-4); the sole-writer claim is made explicit under overlap (FR-5); observable order is pinned even though execution order becomes wall-clock (FR-6); `PARTIAL` routing is recorded at the inner join and taken at the outer join (FR-7); Step 3j gains a second barrier on inner-join completion (FR-8); the contract-amendment loop's entry point moves to Step 3j so it can never invalidate an in-flight leaf (FR-9); and `max_parallel_lanes` starts counting inner-join integration coders so the ceiling keeps binding (FR-10). FR-8 through FR-10 are hazards ADR-0013 does not name — without them the barrier change ships a race, so they land in the same phase as FR-1.

The change closes with the derived-tree contract: `prime-agent/skills/**` is regenerated by `scripts/build-prime-agent.mjs`, never hand-edited, and ADR-0013 moves `Proposed → Accepted` with its four questions answered in the record and the two stale cross-references corrected.

**Verification is structural and arithmetic, not behavioural.** `parallelism` defaults to `off` in this repo and a `full` run with `k ≥ 2` is not reachable here at all, so **no run in this repository exercises the changed path and no behavioural test can honestly be written.** The plan does not invent one. What it does instead: a grep-shaped assertion set proven red before the edits, hand re-derivation of all four worked examples, the builder's `--check` drift guard, `prime-agent`'s own suite, and the `clean-code-gates` suite at its current 213-passing baseline as a no-regression floor.

## Technical Notes

- **The hard constraint (ADR-0013, and `references/config.md`'s own worked example): change the barrier and the charge together, or neither.** This is why Phase 2 is a single large phase rather than two tidy ones. Splitting `SKILL.md` and `references/config.md` into separate phases would land the gate and the ladder on two accounts transiently — the exact defect the cost side exists to prevent. Do not "helpfully" re-order Phase 2 into sub-phases.
- **Single-source-of-truth references.** The normative cost model lives in `references/config.md`; `SKILL.md` → Step 2p.2 summarizes and prints it. New normative detail belongs in `config.md`; `SKILL.md`'s printed-arithmetic rules are kept in step with it, never restated as a second model.
- **Mirror machinery — the strongest wording constraint.** The inner-join level is repriced in exactly the shape the Step 2s architect level already uses (charged once, on the first adoption, at slowest-of-`k`), reusing that bullet's phrasing. Do not invent a parallel formulation.
- **Recorded history is kept, not deleted.** `references/config.md`'s established convention preserves every corrected defect on record (the `min(leaf)` comparand note, the integration-slice note, the gate-vs-ladder example). The superseded `(k − 1) × J` paragraph is preserved with an ADR-0013 supersession note — noting that the ADR-0012-era charge was correct for the barrier discipline in force at the time.
  - **Resolved tension the coder must respect:** AC-1 forbids the literal `k × J` anywhere, while FR-11 preserves the superseded rationale. These are compatible only if the history note expresses the superseded quantity as `(k − 1) × J` and in prose ("charged once per adopted lane, serialized behind the leaf barrier") and never re-introduces the literal `k × J`.
